Privacy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Nothing about you is kept

Pisteday has no accounts and keeps no personal data: no name, email address, date of birth or location, and no tracking or advertising cookies.

What stays on your phone

Your preferences (piste colours, pace, lifts and pistes you avoid, favourite ski areas), your saved routes and the ski area data are kept in your browser's storage on your own phone, so the app works offline on the mountain. They are never sent to us. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or deleting the app from your home screen, removes them.

Your location, when you use "near me" or start from where you are, is used on your phone only and never leaves it.

Hosting

The app, the ski area data and the live lift status are served by GitHub (GitHub, Inc.: GitHub Pages and raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any website host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement. Pisteday itself does not see or keep these logs.
