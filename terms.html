The service

Pisteday plans routes over the lifts and pistes of ski areas. It is free while it is being tested, and needs no account.

A guide, not a guarantee

Routes, times, distances, lift status and opening hours come from open map data (OpenStreetMap) and other sources, and can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Always follow the signs, the piste markings, closures and the instructions of the ski area and its staff, and ski within your ability. Off-piste and closed pistes are never part of a route. We are not liable for damage arising from use of the routes, except in case of intent or gross negligence.
